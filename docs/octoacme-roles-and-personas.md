# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural direction, technical feasibility, and cross-team implementation decisions. They partner with Product, Project, and Engineering stakeholders to ensure the solution is practical, maintainable, and aligned to the delivery plan.

### Responsibilities
- Define and guide technical architecture and design direction
- Evaluate feasibility, dependencies, and implementation trade-offs
- Coordinate technical planning with developers, PMs, and stakeholders
- Help identify and mitigate engineering risks that could affect scope or delivery
- Support decision-making on technical standards, integration points, and quality constraints

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that support business outcomes
- Reduce implementation ambiguity and rework
- Keep delivery aligned with architecture, quality, and security expectations

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Planning and dependency alignment with PM and Product stakeholders
- Escalation of technical risks and blockers in standups and governance meetings

---

## QA / Quality Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Quality Leads ensure that work meets the agreed acceptance criteria, quality bar, and release readiness expectations. They help establish validation strategies and coordinate quality checks across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy, testing approach, and coverage requirements
- Review acceptance criteria and clarify validation expectations
- Coordinate test planning, regression coverage, and release readiness checks
- Identify quality risks and defects early in delivery
- Partner with developers and PMs to confirm readiness for release

### Goals
- Improve confidence in product quality and customer experience
- Reduce defects and rework before release
- Support predictable, low-risk delivery

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria reviews and test planning
- QA sign-off checkpoints and release readiness discussions
- Defect triage and risk escalation with engineering and PM stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads protect the project from avoidable risk by ensuring compliance, privacy, and security concerns are addressed early and consistently. They help the team balance delivery speed with responsible governance.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify relevant controls, dependencies, and risk considerations
- Ensure mitigation plans are documented and tracked
- Partner with engineering and project leadership on risk assessment
- Support incident and escalation workflows when risk or compliance concerns arise

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Align project decisions with organizational standards
- Support sustainable, trustworthy delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and risk assessments
- Security sign-off checkpoints and incident escalations
- Coordination with PM, Product, and engineering leaders on mitigation actions

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Stakeholder Representatives ensure the project remains anchored to customer needs, adoption goals, and stakeholder expectations. They help translate external realities into product and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Represent the voice of the customer and key stakeholders
- Surface adoption, usability, and experience expectations
- Align project outcomes with stakeholder needs and business priorities
- Contribute feedback to roadmap, backlog, and release decisions
- Support stakeholder communication and readiness activities

### Goals
- Improve customer impact and adoption
- Ensure stakeholder expectations are visible and managed
- Strengthen alignment between delivery and business value

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and feedback loops
- Roadmap and release reviews
- Customer-facing issue surfacing and business impact discussions

---

## How these personas interact with existing roles

- Developers work with Technical Leads on architecture, technical trade-offs, and implementation quality.
- Product Managers collaborate with Customer Success / Stakeholder Representatives to ensure customer and business needs remain visible in prioritization and roadmap decisions.
- Project Managers coordinate the work across these roles to manage timelines, dependencies, decision-making, and communication.
- QA / Quality Leads partner with Developers and PMs to confirm acceptance criteria and release readiness.
- Security / Compliance Leads partner with Engineering, PM, and Product stakeholders to address risk and compliance matters earlier in the process.
- These roles are not substitutes for the existing personas; they add accountability and specialization where cross-functional project work requires it.
